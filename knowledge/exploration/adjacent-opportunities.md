## Fly ash — construction materials opportunity (India)

Fly ash (a coal-combustion by-product) is a **supplementary cementitious material** used in
cement/concrete. Export-grade fly ash must be *classified* (fine-particle separation) to
conform to quality standards — the exact data/quality-provenance problem our platform
addresses for PCF and recycled content. Indian exporters are pulled into EU-facing supply
chains as construction products and cement fall under ESPR.

### Reference companies (India, export-grade fly ash classification)

## Ashtech India

- **Type:** Company (fly ash classification / export)
- **Summary:** Major domestic + international player handling millions of tonnes of ash.
  Processes and classifies fine-particle fly ash to conform strictly to **BS EN 450
  Category S** (ultra-fine European standard) and **ASTM C 618 Class F**.
- **Relevance to DPP / opportunity:** Example of quality-classification + certification
  workflow that must be traceable and documentable; a target customer profile for the
  harmonization/verification layer.

### Opportunity notes

- The classification workflow (raw ash → graded product → export pack) mirrors the
  multi-tier aggregation + compliance-math problem in our core engine.
- Standards to track: **BS EN 450** (fly ash for concrete — Category S = ultra-fine) and
  **ASTM C 618** (Class F). These are the vertical's "schema".
- Construction products / cement are covered by the **revised Construction Products
  Regulation (CPR)** which mandates a construction DPP (in force 7 Jan 2025; applicable
  8 Jan 2026; phased per product family 2026–2032) — see `../research/regulations.md`.

## Construction-materials DPP — regulatory context (CPR + ESPR)

- **Type:** Regulation
- **Summary:** The revised CPR (2024) requires a DPP for construction products (CE marking
  linkage), running parallel to ESPR. Construction has among the most concrete timelines
  after batteries; obligations phase in per product family 2026–2032.
- **Relevance to DPP / opportunity:** Fly ash (as cement/concrete input) sits inside this
  category. The fly-ash classification + quality-certification workflow (BS EN 450 /
  ASTM C 618) becomes passport-relevant data.
