# Understanding Check — CBAM (reusable FAQ)

A self-check and quick-reference for pitching/answering questions. Keep these distinctions sharp;
they're what separates sounding informed from being wrong.

## The three precise clauses (always attach these)
1. **Annual submission** — declaration + certificate surrender by **30 Sep** for the *prior*
   calendar year (retrospective by design).
2. **Quarterly provisioning** — from 2027, hold certificates covering **≥50% of YTD emissions**
   (this is *not* a data submission).
3. **Accredited third-party verification** — actual values need a **partner accredited verifier**
   (the stamp is never ours — independence rule).

## Right vs precise

| Common phrasing | Precise version |
|---|---|
| "CBAM tax on exporters" | Carbon charge (**certificates**) on the **importer**; exporter *provides verifiable data*. |
| "Quarterly/weekly submission" | **Annual** legal submission; quarterly = certificate holding; frequent **capture** is practice, not filing. |
| "Verifier approves for submission" | Verifier **attests** (verification report); **importer submits**. |
| "Verifier checks regularly" | Legally **annual per reporting year**; rolling assurance is a service innovation. |
| "Forensic check of everything" | **Materiality-based, sampled, reasonable assurance** — but omissions/misstatements are explicitly targeted. |
| "Exporter" as the unit | **Installation** is the unit; one exporter may run several. |
| Default by installation | Defaults are **per CN code + country**. |

## Quick FAQ

**Who is liable?** The EU importer (authorised CBAM declarant).
**How often is data submitted?** Annually (by 30 Sep for the prior year). Quarterly = certificate holding.
**Must exporters open their whole business?** No — scoped disclosure of emissions data/evidence
to the registry/verifier, with confidentiality. Prove it or pay defaults.
**Who verifies?** An **accredited, independent** verifier (Art. 8). It validates the data; we
deliver *verifier-ready*.
**Are actuals always cheaper?** Usually (defaults are punitive), but the real rule is: actuals need
verification; defaults are the fallback.
**What happens if the verifier finds a material misstatement?** Actuals can't be used as-is →
remediate or fall back toward defaults.

## The accounting analogy (accurate, with caveats)
- Annual retrospective filing + records kept in-year + a professional prepares + a validator
  assures + one authority receives it + errors cost money.
- **Caveat:** the assurance is an **audit**, not a review; the base is **technical (emissions
  engineering)**, not financial; and the source data is partly held by a **third party** (the
  non-EU supplier), which is the coordination gap the product solves.

## Accounting role mapping

| Tax world | CBAM world |
|---|---|
| SME bookkeeper | the mill's data capture |
| Accounting practice (prep) | consultant / service provider (us) |
| Statutory auditor | accredited verifier (partner) |
| Tax authority | EU DPP/**CBAM Registry** / NCA |

## Enterprise trap to avoid in a pitch
Never say "we are the verifier" or "we approve submissions." Say: **"we deliver verifier-ready
data and prepare the filing; the accredited partner verifier stamps it; the importer submits."**
