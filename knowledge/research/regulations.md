# Regulations

EU laws, deadlines, and obligations that drive the Digital Product Passport.

## ESPR — Ecodesign for Sustainable Products Regulation

- **Type:** Regulation
- **Summary:** Framework regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/1781) that establishes the
  Digital Product Passport as the tool for sharing product sustainability information
  across categories. The Commission defines data requirements per product group.
- **Relevance to DPP:** Legal basis for all category passports beyond batteries.
  The Commission is establishing a Semantic Repository as the common reference for DPP data.
- **Notes:** Data requirements per category are set through delegated acts.
> Source: Renoon — https://renoon.com — article — 30 Jul 2026

## EU Battery Regulation 2023/1542

- **Type:** Regulation
- **Summary:** First binding DPP mandate. Requires a "Battery Passport" for EV batteries,
  Light Means of Transport (LMT) batteries, and industrial batteries above 2 kWh placed
  on the EU single market.
- **Relevance to DPP:** Drives the **18 February 2027** mandatory submission date — the
  first live DPP deadline. Applies to every economic operator placing qualifying batteries
  in the EU, regardless of where the company is based (Germany, UK, India, US, etc.).
- **Notes:** Also requires a QR code on the product, data on carbon footprint and recycled content.
> Source: GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk — gov — 15 Jun 2026
> Source: single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu — Digital Product Passport — gov — 15 Aug 2026

## February 2027 mandatory submission (first wave)

- **Type:** Deadline
- **Summary:** From 18 Feb 2027, battery passports become mandatory. Legal entities
  responsible are "Economic Operators" (manufacturers, importers, distributors).
- **Relevance to DPP:** First enforceable date; other product categories follow in waves
  through 2027-2030+.
- **Notes:** Compliance trickles down the supply chain — OEMs must demand and verify
  supplier data (origin, carbon footprint, recycled content), forcing mid-tier refiners
  and scrap processors to digitize.
> Source: GOV.UK — https://www.gov.uk — gov — 15 Jun 2026
> Source: MoveTheNeedle.news — https://movetheneedle.news — article — 21 Aug 2026

## Article 65 — second-life / remanufactured batteries

- **Type:** Obligation
- **Summary:** A company that collects, repairs, or repurposes a used battery (e.g. into a
  solar storage unit) is legally treated as a new manufacturer. It must update the central
  EU registry or mint a new passport.
- **Relevance to DPP:** Brings second-life repurposers (UK, India, Belgium, etc.) into scope.

## EU DPP Registry & Semantic Repository

- **Type:** Infrastructure
- **Summary:** Central EU DPP Registry stores passports; a Semantic Repository acts as the
  common reference for DPP data definitions across categories.
- **Relevance to DPP:** Every submission flows here. Designed for uniform machine-readable
  data models and documented APIs across Europe.
> Source: Renoon — https://renoon.com — article — 30 Jul 2026
> Source: single-market-economy.ec.europa.eu — Digital Product Passport — gov — 15 Aug 2026

## Construction Products Regulation (CPR, revised) — construction DPP

- **Type:** Regulation
- **Summary:** The revised EU CPR (2024) introduces a **dedicated DPP for construction
  products**, parallel to ESPR. Entered into force 7 Jan 2025; generally applicable
  8 Jan 2026. DPP obligations and climate-impact reporting phase in **per product family
  between 2026 and 2032**.
- **Relevance to DPP:** The legal vehicle for fly ash / cement / concrete and other
  construction materials (a natural fit for the adjacent fly-ash opportunity in
  `../exploration/adjacent-opportunities.md`). DPP becomes mandatory for CE marking.
- **Notes:** Construction is the sector with the most concrete timelines after batteries.
> Source: eudigitalproductpassport.org — Construction products digital product passport — article — 2026
> Source: constructionproducts.org.uk — EU CPR - Digital Product Passports — article — 2026

## ESPR delegated acts — confirmed timeline (per product group)

- **Type:** Timeline
- **Summary:** ESPR First Working Plan 2025–2030 (COM(2025) 187, adopted 16 Apr 2025) governs
  which products get delegated acts and when. Adoption dates are **indicative, not binding**;
  compliance = adoption + **at least 18 months transition** (Art. 4(4) ESPR). Mid-term review 2028.
  **No ESPR delegated act has been adopted as of Sept 2026.**
- **Draft timeline (delegated-act adoption → est. obligation):**
  - **Iron & steel** (intermediate): **Q4 2026** → ~2028 — the *first* ESPR category.
  - **Textiles & apparel** (final): Q3–Q4 2027 → ~2029. **Footwear & accessories excluded**
    from the first textiles act — separate study pending (do not build for footwear now).
  - **Aluminium** (intermediate): Q3–Q4 2027 → ~2029.
  - **Tyres:** 2027; **Furniture:** 2028; **Mattresses:** 2029 (~2030–2031).
  - **Electronics/ICT:** no standalone DA — horizontal EEE recyclability measure (2029) +
    energy-related products track (mobile/tablets end 2030).
- **Registry milestones:** DPP Registry framework + six DPP standards (Jul 2026); Registry
  operational 20 Jul 2026; remaining two standards Sept 2026; DA for battery access rights Q4 2026.
- **Data overlaps:** iron & steel and aluminium (intermediate products) both hinge on carbon
  intensity + recycled content — data largely overlaps **CBAM/ETS** reporting, giving
  operators a head start.

## In-force legislation with DPP-related data (the near-term wedges)

These are binding NOW and force the same data pipelines the future DPP acts will reuse —
the commercial wedge into steel/aluminium and textiles customers years before their DPP.

- **Type:** Regulation
- **CBAM definitive phase (Reg 2023/956):** LIVE since **1 Jan 2026**. EU importers of
  iron & steel, aluminium, cement, fertilisers, electricity, hydrogen must be Authorised
  CBAM Declarants and report **verified embedded emissions**; certificate purchase/surrender
  from 2027 (first surrender ~30 Sep 2027). Not a DPP — but produces the per-tonne carbon +
  supplier data the steel/aluminium DPP will reuse. **Metals customers pay for this now.**
- **ESPR unsold-products destruction ban + disclosure (Reg (EU) 2026/2, acts adopted 9 Feb
  2026):** IN FORCE **19 Jul 2026** for large enterprises placing apparel, clothing
  accessories and **footwear** — no destruction of unsold stock; disclosure of unsold
  volumes (2025 volumes reported in 2026). ESPR's first operative sector obligation; medium
  companies phase in 2030. **Textile/footwear large brands need data systems now.**
- **Battery Regulation 2023/1542:** the only live *DPP proper*; registry + data model
  operational, mandate 18 Feb 2027. Working reference for data model + registry behaviour.
- **ETS:** verified steel emissions data (ongoing head-start for DPP carbon fields).
- **CSRD:** value-chain sustainability reporting in force for large companies (FY2024–25+).
- **CPR (construction):** applicable since Jan 2026, construction DPP phasing 2026–2032.
- **PPWR (packaging):** technical-file obligation Aug 2026; packaging DPP track live 2026.

**Watch item — DPP Service Provider Delegated Act (Q2–Q3 2027):** regulates third-party DPP
service providers (our SaaS role). May impose registration and **portability** duties —
relevant to the EO-owned-URL-namespace design principle (`architecture.md`).

(definitive calculation) · IR (EU) 2025/2546 (verification) · IR (EU) 2025/2621 (default values)
· IR (EU) 2025/2620 (benchmarks).

**Calc-input list (canonical, feeds C2):**
- Installation identity: country, UN/LOCODE, production route + route-qualifying parameters.
- Reporting period + production activity level (tonnes) + CN code.
- Source streams: quantity, unit (t/TJ/Nm³/MWh), NCV, emission factor (tCO₂/TJ), oxidation factor.
- Electricity: MWh + emission factor (grid or contractual PPA/GO), plus own-generation/heat.
- Precursors: mass × SEE per covered input.
- Result: direct tCO₂, indirect tCO₂, SEE tCO₂e/t, determination (actual vs default).
