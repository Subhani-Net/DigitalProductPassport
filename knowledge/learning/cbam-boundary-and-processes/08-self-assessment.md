# Module 8 — Self-Assessment

Answer before opening the key. Aim for 9/12.

## Part A — Facts vs conclusions
1. Turn “our plant is efficient” into two fact requests.
2. Why is a conclusion dangerous for verification?
3. What five things should every fact have?

## Part B — Processes
4. Name the main steps in a BF-BOF route, in order.
5. What is the key difference between BF-BOF and EAF inputs?
6. In primary aluminium, what causes PFC emissions, and what records do you ask for?
7. What is extrusion’s main input and output?

## Part C — Energy, gases, meters
8. What are BFG, BOFG and COG, and why must they be recorded?
9. Why can’t you treat SCM and Nm³ as the same?
10. What does a meter map contain?

## Part D — Boundary and documents
11. What is the difference between the legal entity and the installation?
12. Which document is closest to what a verifier expects, and what must you check about it?

## Answer key

1. Energy per tonne + the utility bills; throughput + the production log. *(Any two fact requests.)*
2. It cannot be traced to a source, unit, period or owner, so it cannot be audited or reproduced.
3. Source, unit, period, owner, and how it is measured (plus the artefact).
4. Coke oven → sinter → blast furnace → BOF → ladle furnace → casting → reheat → rolling →
   coating (pickling/cold rolling as applicable).
5. BF-BOF uses iron ore/coke/hot metal; EAF uses scrap/DRI and electricity (electrodes).
6. **Anode effects** in the Hall-Héroult cell; ask for anode-effect count/frequency/duration,
   cell technology and the PFC factor source.
7. Input: **billet**; output: **profiles** (plus scrap/butt ends).
8. Blast-furnace gas, BOF gas, coke-oven gas — they are fuels/transfers; missing them causes
   double-counting or omission.
9. They use different reference conditions/bases; convert with a documented basis/density/NCV.
10. Meter ID, stream, location, unit, range/class, calibration, frequency, owner, data system.
11. Entity = company; installation = the technical site/unit under one operator. One entity can
    have several installations; one site can host tenants.
12. The **monitoring plan**; check its date/version, boundary, route and period match the pilot.

## Field exercise

For a plant you know (or the synthetic docket), produce:
1. A one-page **process flow** with inputs/outputs.
2. A **fence-line sketch** with shared utilities.
3. A **fuel/gas list** with meters.
4. A **meter map** with calibration dates.
5. Five **“show me”** questions you would ask next.

**Pass bar:** every item has an artefact or is marked Unknown in the gap register.
