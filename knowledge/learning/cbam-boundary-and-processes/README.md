# CBAM Boundary & Processes — Education Pack

**Level:** practitioner · **Use with:** `business/pilot/phase3-input-validation-guide.md` and the
Phase 3 templates.

**Why this pack exists:** to map a plant you must collect **facts**, not conclusions. “We are
efficient” is a conclusion; a meter reading, a bill, a process diagram and a unit are facts. This
pack teaches the processes, the data and the questions that produce facts.

## Outcomes

By the end you can:
1. Explain **facts vs conclusions** and convert vague answers into evidence.
2. Read a **process flow / site layout** and identify the steps.
3. Name the main **steel and aluminium process steps** and their inputs/outputs.
4. Recognise **fuels, gas lines and reduction agents** (NG, BFG, BOFG, COG, H₂, coke/coal/PCI).
5. Distinguish **on-site generation** from imported/exported heat and electricity.
6. Build a **meter map** (what, where, unit, calibration).
7. Separate the **legal entity** from the **installation** (fence line, co-located tenants,
   shared utilities).
8. Identify **existing documents** and **production routes + qualifying parameters**.

## The one rule

> **Ask “show me”, not “tell me”.** If it is not in a document, a meter, a diagram or a dated
> record, it is not a fact yet.

## Tagging

**Fact** = cited regulation/standard · **Typical** = industry norm, verify per plant ·
**[verify]** = confirm against the instrument or the plant. Numbers marked *illustrative* are
shapes, not truths.
