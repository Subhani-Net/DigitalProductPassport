# Module 7 — Field Question Bank (Facts, Not Conclusions)

Use these verbatim. For every answer, ask for the **artefact**: document, meter, diagram, dated
record. Record “Unknown” as a fact; do not fill it with an assumption.

## 1. Process flow / site layout

- “Can you show me a process flow diagram — even hand-drawn?”
- “Can you mark the **fence line** on a site plan?”
- “Which steps happen **on this site**, and which happen elsewhere?”
- “Where do materials enter and leave the site?”
- “Who else operates on this site?”

## 2. Process list

- “Which of these exist here: coke oven, sinter, pellet, blast furnace, BOF, EAF, ladle furnace,
  casting, reheat, hot rolling, cold rolling, pickling, coating, forging, extrusion, anodising?”
- “For each one, what goes in and what comes out?”
- “Which steps are currently idle or mothballed?”

## 3. Inputs / outputs per step

- “For [step], show me the input quantities and the output quantities for the period.”
- “What by-products come out — slag, scale, trim, dust, dross?”
- “What gases come out, and where do they go?”
- “What is the yield between input and output?”

## 4. Fuel and gas lines

- “Which fuels are burned on site, and where?” (coal, coke, PCI, NG, diesel, fuel oil, LPG)
- “Do you recover **BFG, BOFG or COG**? Show me the meter and destination.”
- “Do you flare or vent any gas? Show me the records.”
- “What reduction agents go into the furnace?” (coke, coal, PCI, gas, H₂)
- “What are the rates?” (kg/t hot metal, etc.)

## 5. On-site generation and imported/exported energy

- “Do you generate electricity on site? With what fuel?”
- “Show me the generation meter and the fuel logs.”
- “Do you import steam or heat? From whom?”
- “Do you export electricity, steam or heat? To whom, and how is it metered?”
- “Do you have a PPA or Guarantees of Origin? Show the contract and the MWh.”

## 6. Meter map

- “For each stream, which meter measures it?”
- “Where is the meter, what unit, and how often is it read?”
- “When was it last calibrated? Show the certificate.”
- “Who owns and maintains the meter?”
- “Where is the reading stored?” (log, ERP, SCADA)

## 7. Entity vs installation

- “What is the legal entity, and what is the installation?”
- “Is any utility shared with another company on site?”
- “Who owns the captive power plant, steam line, weighbridge, water treatment?”
- “Are there co-located tenants? What do they make?”
- “What crosses the fence line, and who meters it?”

## 8. Existing documents

- “Do you have a monitoring plan? Show me the version and date.”
- “Any ETS, BRSR, ISO 50001, GHG (ISO 14064-1), EPD or PCF documents?”
- “For each, what boundary does it use?”
- “Do you have calibration certificates, mill certificates, CoAs?”
- “Can you share customs/sales invoices showing CN codes?”

## 9. Production route + qualifying parameters

- “Which route: BF-BOF, EAF, DRI, primary Al, secondary Al, extrusion?”
- “What are the route-qualifying values?” (hot-metal ratio, coke rate, PCI, scrap ratio, DRI share,
  cell technology)
- “What record supports each value?”
- “Has the route changed during the period?”

## Red flags → follow-up

| You hear | Ask next |
|---|---|
| “We’re efficient.” | “Show me energy per tonne and the bills.” |
| “We don’t have that stream.” | “Show me the process flow / meter list; who would know?” |
| “The consultant has it.” | “Can you request the document and share it?” |
| “Roughly…” | “What is the exact reading and unit?” |
| “It’s green electricity.” | “Show the PPA/GO and the MWh.” |
| “We buy some scrap.” | “Invoices and weighbridge for the period.” |
| “Same as last year.” | “Show last year’s report and this year’s data.” |

## One-page field checklist

- [ ] Process flow diagram
- [ ] Site plan + fence line
- [ ] Process list (on-site vs upstream)
- [ ] Inputs/outputs per step (with by-products/gases)
- [ ] Fuel list + invoices
- [ ] Gas meters + destinations (BFG/BOFG/COG/flare)
- [ ] Reduction agents + rates
- [ ] On-site generation + fuel logs
- [ ] Import/export of electricity/heat + contracts
- [ ] Meter map + calibration certificates
- [ ] Entity vs installation; shared utilities; tenants
- [ ] Monitoring plan / ETS / BRSR / ISO 50001 / GHG / EPD
- [ ] Production route + qualifying parameters
- [ ] Production logs + sales/customs invoices (CN codes)
