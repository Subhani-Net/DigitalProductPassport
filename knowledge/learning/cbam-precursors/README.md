# CBAM Precursors & Timeline — Focused Course

**Level:** Practitioner+ (assumes CBAM 201) · **Prereq:** `../cbam-201/05-precursors-and-complex-goods.md`

**Why this course exists:** two things trip people up more than anything else:
1. **Precursors** — most steel/aluminium goods are *complex*, so the biggest part of the number
   comes from upstream inputs, not the final plant. Get the precursor wrong and the whole report
   is wrong.
2. **The timeline** — everyone files by **30 Sep** for the *prior* year, which makes it sound like
   everyone calculates at the same moment. They don't. There are **two clocks**: capture during
   the year, declare after it.

## Outcomes
By the end you can:
1. Decide whether a good is **simple or complex**, and list its CBAM-covered precursors.
2. Write and apply **`EE_inpMat = Σ(Mᵢ × SEEᵢ)`** correctly (consumption, yield, recursion).
3. Send a **precursor data request** that a supplier can actually answer — and validate the reply.
4. Explain the **two clocks** and walk a customer through **CY2026 → 30 Sep 2027** without confusing
   data capture, verification, declaration and certificate holding.
5. Handle the **scenario matrix** (verified / unverified / prior-year / multi-installation /
   multi-importer / de minimis).

## The mental model (memorise this)
```
CAPTURE CLOCK  (1 Jan – 31 Dec)        DECLARE CLOCK  (1 Jan – 30 Sep next year)
monitoring plan, meters, invoices      assemble → verify → declare → surrender
supplier data requests                 importer files; exporter supplies; verifier verifies
```
You **calculate after the year**, but you must **capture during the year**. That is the whole
answer to "everyone submits in September — how does that work?"
