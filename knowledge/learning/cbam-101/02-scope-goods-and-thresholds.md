# Module 2 — Scope, Goods & Thresholds

## 2.1 Which goods are covered

CBAM applies to a fixed list of carbon-intensive goods (Annex I of Reg (EU) 2023/956),
identified by **Combined Nomenclature (CN) codes**:

| Sector | What it covers (examples) |
|---|---|
| **Cement** | clinker, Portland cement, aluminous cement |
| **Iron & steel** | sintered ore, pig iron, DRI, ferro-alloys, crude steel, most steel products |
| **Aluminium** | unwrought and most aluminium products |
| **Fertilisers** | ammonia, nitric acid, potash-based fertilisers, etc. |
| **Electricity** | imported electricity |
| **Hydrogen** | hydrogen and derivatives |

**Fact** (Annex I). The exact product list is by **CN code** — scope questions are always
answered by matching the goods' CN code against Annex I.

## 2.2 Precursors — the hidden scope

- Some covered goods are made from **other covered goods** ("precursors"), e.g. crude steel
  from pig iron/DRI, or steel products from crude steel. **Fact**.
- Emissions of a finished good include the **embedded emissions of its precursors** — so scope
  follows the chain, not just the final product. This is why a simple product can carry a
  large carbon footprint from upstream. **Fact**.

## 2.3 The de-minimis threshold (50 tonnes)

- **Importers bringing in ≤ 50 tonnes per year** of CBAM goods are **exempt** (the "Omnibus"
  simplification, Oct 2025). **Fact** (per our KB; confirm current text).
- Consequence: the in-scope population is smaller but higher-value — serious importers only.
  **Claim** (market read, `../../research/goto-market-sizing.md`).

## 2.4 Who is "in scope" — the actor question

- The obligation falls on the **EU importer** of covered goods. **Fact**.
- Where someone else lodges the customs declaration, the **indirect customs representative**
  can be the CBAM actor. **Fact**.
- Non-EU producers are **not** directly obligated under CBAM, but they are the **data origin**
  — and commercially forced to supply it. **Fact + commercial logic**.

## 2.5 Common misconceptions (101-level)
- ❌ "CBAM is a tax on EU producers" → it is a charge on **imports**, mirroring the ETS.
- ❌ "Only the exporter must act" → the **importer** is legally liable; the exporter provides data.
- ❌ "All products from a sector are covered" → only the **listed CN codes** (plus precursors).
- ❌ "Small imports still need to comply" → the **50 t/yr** de-minimis exempts them.
