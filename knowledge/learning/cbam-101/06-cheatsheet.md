# CBAM 101 — Cheatsheet

## One-liner
CBAM = a border carbon charge on imported carbon-intensive goods, mirroring the EU ETS, to
stop carbon leakage. Legal duty sits with the **EU importer**.

## Key facts
- **Law:** Regulation (EU) 2023/956.
- **Transitional:** 1 Oct 2023 – 31 Dec 2025 (reporting only).
- **Definitive (financial):** from **1 Jan 2026**.
- **First certificate surrender:** **30 Sep 2027** (for CY2026).
- **Certificate price:** tracks ETS — Q1 2026 **€75.36/t**, Q2 2026 **€75.28/t**.
- **Certificate sales begin:** 1 Feb 2027 (first surrender 30 Sep 2027).
- **CBAM factor (phase-in):** 2.5% (2026) → 100% (2034).
- **Excess-emissions penalty:** **€100/tCO₂e** (indexed); unauthorised import 3×–5×. (Legacy
  transitional reporting fine was €10–50/t.)
- **De minimis:** **≤ 50 t/yr** cumulative (cement/steel/aluminium/fertilisers; not electricity/H₂).
- **Omnibus:** Regulation (EU) 2025/2083 (in force 20 Oct 2025) — 50 t rule, 30 Sep deadline,
  certificate sales Feb 2027, quarterly holding 50%.
- **Authorised importers (mid-2026):** >12,000 applied, >4,100 authorised (Claim).

## Covered sectors
Cement · Iron & Steel · Aluminium · Fertilisers · Electricity · Hydrogen (by **CN code**,
plus **precursors**).

## Actors
EU importer / **Authorised CBAM Declarant** (liable) · indirect customs rep · non-EU operator
(**O3CI**) · **accredited verifier** · **NCA** · customs · Commission (DG TAXUD) · **DRMC**.

## Registry modules
**AMM** (declarants) · **O3CI** (operators + verifiers) · **DRMC** (monitoring).

## Flow
operator → **verifier** → **Registry** → importer declares → certificates.
(No direct exporter→importer data transfer; Registry is the broker.)

## Emissions essentials
- **SEE** = specific embedded emissions (tCO₂e/t) = **direct + indirect** (+ precursors).
- **Direct:** calculation-based / CEMS / mass-balance.
- **Indirect:** electricity MWh/t × emission factor.
- **Actual values** → need a **verifier's report**; **default values** → punitive fallback.
- Steel routes: BF-BOF / EAF / DRI. Aluminium: primary (electricity + PFC) vs secondary.

## Key implementing acts
IR 2023/1773 (methodology) · IR 2024/3210 (registry) · IR 2025/2547 (calculation) ·
IR 2025/2546 (verification) · Del. 2025/2551 (accreditation) · IR 2025/2621 (defaults).

## Acronyms
CBAM · ETS · CN (Combined Nomenclature) · SEE · NCA · DRMC · AMM · O3CI · EORI · PFC · CEMS ·
MWh/t · tCO₂e/t.
