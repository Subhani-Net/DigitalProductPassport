# CBAM Verifier Accreditation — Role & Self-Study Syllabus

Path for operating as **Operations Subject Matter Expert (SME) / Management Lead** of a CBAM
verification agency: leverage the IT-systems pedigree to build the compliance engine, data
routing, and institutional workflows; hire metallurgical/chemical scientists as technical
partners. Companion to the "Verification accreditation path" in `../business/product-roadmap.md` §2.

**Credential reality check:** the CBAM verifier credential is **ISO 17029 + ISO 14065 + CBAM
Delegated Regulation (EU) 2025/2551** (NAB-granted). ISO 14064-3 is the GHG-verification
competence base; DG TAXUD modules are registry/portal training. Corporate accreditation needs a
documented system + competent **team matrix** + **witnessed audits** → realistic 12–24 months.

## Module 1 — Core legislation (the "what")

- **Regulation (EU) 2023/956** — foundational CBAM law. Read: verifier role, accreditation
  principles, reporting timeline.
- **Implementing Regulation (EU) 2025/2547** (definitive-phase calculation) + **Regulation
  (EU) 2026/1740** [verify — newer act] — exact definitions of **system boundaries** and
  **Specific Embedded Emissions (SEE)** methodology; how **precursors** (inputs already
  carrying embedded carbon) are handled.

## Module 2 — Auditing standards (the "how")

- **ISO 14064-3** — verification/validation of GHG assertions. Master: materiality thresholds,
  reasonable vs limited assurance, inherent risk, control risk, data-sampling plans. Basis for
  workflow templates.
- **ISO 14065 / ISO 17029** — governance of validation/verification bodies. Design the firm's
  **impartiality committee**, conflict-of-interest policy, and the **firewall between Lead
  Auditor and Independent Reviewer**.

## Module 3 — Official Commission training & guidance (the framework)

- **DG TAXUD Guidance on CBAM Verification and Accreditation for Verifiers and NABs** —
  exact templates for a compliant CBAM Verification Report.
- **EU Customs Learning Portal** e-learning: *"Become a CBAM Verifier: All About Accreditation
  and Verification"* — bridges legal text to practical implementation.

## Module 4 — Operational technicalities (the IT edge)

- **CBAM Registry user manuals** — data schema/architecture of the Central CBAM Registry:
  EU Login, how an accredited entity structures and uploads verification files to NCAs.
- **Monitoring Plans (MPs)** — how installations define data-tracking boundaries; the digital
  data blueprint reviewed *before* allocating engineers to a physical plant audit.

## Strategic self-study checklist

- [ ] Download/bind the 3 core EU texts: 2023/956, 2025/2547, and the 2026/1740 update [verify].
