# Companies

Companies in scope of the DPP, grouped by obligation type. The Feb 2027 wave is driven by
the EU Battery Regulation. Category lists below come from one research pass — each section
carries a single shared source citation; individual entries marked `[Unverified]` should be
confirmed before relying on them.

## Global automotive manufacturers (EV OEMs)

Any automaker selling EVs, trucks, or buses in Europe must submit a passport per car battery.

- Volkswagen Group (Audi, Porsche, Cupra)
- Stellantis (Peugeot, Fiat, Citroën, Vauxhall/Opel)
- BMW Group
- Mercedes-Benz Group
- Renault Group
- Volvo Cars — launched the world's first live commercial passport (EX90)
- Tesla Inc.
- Ford Motor Company
- General Motors (GM)
- Rivian Automotive
- BYD Auto
- Geely Holding Group (Polestar, Zeekr)
- NIO
- XPENG
- Hyundai Motor Group (Kia)
- Toyota Motor Corporation
- Honda Motor Co.
- Tata Motors (exports to Europe)
- Mahindra & Mahindra

## Battery cell & pack manufacturers

Provide cells/packs; obligated even if cells cross the EU border inside a device or vehicle.

- LG Energy Solution, Samsung SDI, SK On (South Korea)
- CATL, BYD (FinDreams Battery), CALB, Gotion High-tech, EVE Energy (China)
- Panasonic Energy Co. (Japan)
- Northvolt AB, ACC (Automotive Cells Company — Stellantis, Mercedes, TotalEnergies), Verkor (Europe)

## Micromobility & LMT brands

Feb 2027 explicitly covers LMT batteries (e-bikes, e-scooters, cargo fleets).

- Lime, Voi Technology, Tier Mobility, VanMoof, Cowboy, Rad Power Bikes, Giant Manufacturing
- Drive systems: Bosch eBike Systems, Shimano Inc., Bafang Electric

## Industrial equipment & energy storage (ESS)

Stationary battery systems for grid, telecom backup, solar, or material handling above 2 kWh.

- Tesla (Megapack / Powerwall division)
- Fluence Energy, Wärtsilä Energy, Saft Batteries (TotalEnergies), BYD Energy Storage
- Jungheinrich AG, Kion Group (Linde Material Handling), Caterpillar Inc. (electric equipment in Europe)

## Second-life repurposers & remanufacturers

Legally treated as new manufacturers under Article 65; must update or mint passports.

- Connected Energy (UK), Octave (Belgium), Carvat (India/Europe), Lohum Cleantech (India)

## Supply-chain vendors pulled into compliance (trickle-down)

OEMs force suppliers to digitize data:

- UK pre-treatment/shredders: Altilium, Ecowaste, regional scrap processors (black mass data)
- Indian chemical refiners: Attero Recycling, Lohum, Rubamin (recycled-content certificates
  for lithium carbonate / nickel sulphate)
