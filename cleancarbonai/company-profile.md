**Reading note:** these make CleanCarbon claim in-house **verified & validated CBAM
reports** (ISO 14065 / 17029 are the standards for verification bodies; filenames show a
"UK" issuing scheme for two of them). Certificate ≠ accreditation by a national
accreditation body — treat as vendor-claimed until the PDFs are inspected. This is directly
relevant to our compliance-matrix operating model (see `../compliance-matrix.md` §3).
