## Threat rubric (scored against OUR moat, not market size)

A player scores High threat when it overlaps multiple of OUR differentiators:

1. **Wedge adjacency** — serves the CBAM/emissions need we monetise first (M2, ~Jan 2027).
2. **DPP sell-twice bridge** — carries data beyond the CBAM report toward the EU DPP
   Registry / passport (our structural differentiator).
3. **Supplier-side reach** — tools for non-EU suppliers (India/UK/etc.), multi-tier data.
4. **Standards & evidence depth** — CELEX-traceable schemas, JSON-LD/AAS/GS1, audit trails,
   verification interop (our compliance-matrix moat).

- **High:** overlaps 3–4 dimensions, moving in our verticals (steel/aluminium/textiles).
- **Medium:** overlaps 1–2 dimensions or adjacent geography.
- **Low:** complementary/ecosystem, or commodity open-source with no commercial push.
- **Watch:** not yet in our arena but signaling entry.
