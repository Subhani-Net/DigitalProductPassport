# Competition → Roadmap Mapping

How the landscape findings change (or confirm) our path. Roadmap stays untouched unless a
finding materially changes strategy — these are the candidates and conclusions.

## Confirmed by the landscape

1. **The wedge is contested but winnable.** CarbonChain (both sides), SAP (declarants),
   CleanCarbon (India service) fight M2. Nobody combines: CBAM-first for metals exporters +
   supplier-side reach + CELEX-traceable evidence + DPP bridge. That combination is our
   slot.
2. **The DPP bridge is already being raced (batteries).** Minespider submits to the live EU
   DPP Registry from its app. Metals/textiles (our verticals) remain open — urgency to lock
   the bridge in C6/P2, not P4.
3. **Accredited verification is scarce** (SGS/BV are applicants). Verifier-ready data is a
   wedge property; TÜV SÜD (NABCB India) is the partner benchmark for the supplier side.
4. **Commodity DPP generators ($29–399/mo) set the price floor** and validate SME demand —
   reinforces "adopt the commodity, build the complex layer".

## White space (our slot, restated)

CBAM wedge → **metals, both sides, evidence-deep, verifier-ready** (vs CarbonChain's
commodities breadth, SAP's declarant-only, CleanCarbon's India service) → then the
**DPP sell-twice bridge to the EU Registry for steel/aluminium** (vs Minespider's
battery-first bridge) → with **n-tier mass-balance + CELEX-traceable trust + zero-exposure
attestation** (vs Circularise's mass balance without the wedge, Circulor's
battery/minerals focus). Self-serve product + partner services (Xero model) keeps the
price/automation edge against service-led incumbents.
