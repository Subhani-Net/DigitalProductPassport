# Dossier — CarbonChain (Arena A, HIGH threat)

- **What:** commodity supply-chain carbon platform: "compare supplier carbon costs, get
  compliant, reduce carbon taxes." Products: Supply chain emissions, **CBAM for declarants**,
  **CBAM for installations**, Product carbon footprints, Scope 1/2/3.
- **CBAM depth (unique):** both sides productized — declarants (EU importers) and
  installations (non-EU producers); "reports that follow EU templates and methodologies";
  explains cost formula (emissions intensity × rate − carbon price paid abroad); "Default
  values are punitive, actual values will be required"; "Submit annual CBAM declarations
  through the CBAM Registry."
- **Verification signal:** "Methodology validated against the GHG Protocol by SGS";
  "AI and human in the loop verification for every data point"; ISO 27001.
- **Supplier side:** asset-level data for commodity suppliers; PCF "beyond CBAM… GHG
  Protocol aligned".
- **DPP:** NOT FOUND.
- **Integrations:** "importing your data from ERP, CTRM, Excel files and via APIs" (no
  named vendors).
- **Claims:** "11,000+ commodity suppliers asset-level data"; "500+ CBAM declarations
  completed"; "2bn tonnes CO₂".
- **Threat read:** strongest direct competitor for our M2 CBAM wedge, both sides. Weaknesses:
  no DPP bridge, no India services layer, methodology details behind SGS validation rather
  than open evidence.
