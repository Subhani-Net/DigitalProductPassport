# Dossier — Circularise (Arena B, HIGH threat on our moat layer)

- **What:** "Supply chain traceability software for faster compliance." Three modules:
  1. **Collect** — n-tier supplier data; 2. **Trace** — auditable **Mass Balance /
  Chain of Custody**; 3. **Share** — publish Digital Product Passports, permissioned
  disclosures tied to identifiers and QR/data carrier.
- **Standards:** "Standard GS1 Digital Link. Identifier GTIN + serial"; "Use standard GS1
  identifiers that are not tied to a proprietary database."
- **Integrations:** "ERP/PLM API Integrations"; "API Disclosure Layer allows your customers'
  ERP and PLM systems to pull data automatically"; RESTful API-first; SSO/SAML; REACH/SCIP
  inputs. Pricing page names SAP, Oracle, Microsoft Dynamics for ERP integration add-ons.
- **Use cases:** Battery Passport (EU Battery Reg), CRM Passport, DPP (ESPR), EURO7 EVP,
  ELV, ISCC. Customers cited: Honda, Samsonite, Teijin, Mitsubishi Chemical.
- **Claims:** ISO 27001 certified, 99.9% uptime.
- **CBAM:** NOT FOUND (carbon footprint + methodology evidence as battery-passport data).
- **Threat read:** closest to our "mass-balance + n-tier supplier data" moat. Gaps: no CBAM
  wedge, no EU-registry submission found, no CELEX-traceable evidence disclosure. We must
  differentiate on the metals CBAM-first entry + registry/DPP bridge.
