## Catena-X & Manufacturing-X ecosystem

- **Type:** Consortium / data-space governance
- **Summary:** Catena-X (automotive) runs an operational certification architecture via CABs
  (e.g. TÜV Rheinland): SAMM/BAM semantic validation, EDC interop, TISAX L2, plus the
  Tractus-X DPP Verification add-on. Manufacturing-X is the German umbrella reusing the same
  tech across verticals (Material-X/Base Metals for steel & aluminium; Tex-X for textiles).
- **Relevance to DPP:** Certification paths are a structured blueprint for building compliant
  software; the ecosystem is a community/support route (e.g. Catena-X Readiness Booster).
