# Module 1 — Context & Legal Framework

## 1.1 Why CBAM exists

- The EU decarbonises its own industry through the **EU Emissions Trading System (ETS)**,
  which puts a price on CO₂ emitted inside the EU. **Fact** (Reg 2003/87, ETS Directive).
- If only EU producers pay that carbon cost, production can shift to countries without a
  carbon price — **carbon leakage** — which cuts EU competitiveness *and* does not reduce
  global emissions. **Fact** (commercial rationale).
- **CBAM = Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism**: a border charge on the **embedded carbon** of
  carbon-intensive goods imported into the EU, mirroring the carbon cost EU producers face.
  The aim: equalise the carbon price between domestic and imported goods, and prevent leakage.
  **Fact** (Reg (EU) 2023/956, recitals).
- CBAM sits inside the **European Green Deal / "Fit for 55"** package. **Fact**.

## 1.2 CBAM and the ETS — the mirror

| Aspect | EU ETS (domestic) | CBAM (imports) |
|---|---|---|
| Who pays | EU installations (via ETS allowances) | **Importers** (via CBAM certificates) |
| Price basis | ETS auction price | Tracks the ETS price (weekly average) |
| Free allocation | Phase down over 2026–2034 | CBAM phases **in** in step, so the two stay aligned |
| Scope | EU production | Imports of covered goods |

**Key mental model:** CBAM is the *external mirror* of the ETS. As free ETS allowances to EU
industry shrink, CBAM's charge on imports grows — they are designed to move together.
**Fact** (CBAM ↔ ETS phase-in alignment is explicit in the legislation).

## 1.3 The legal instrument and periodisation

- **Primary law: Regulation (EU) 2023/956** — the CBAM Regulation. **Fact**.
- **Transitional period: 1 Oct 2023 → 31 Dec 2025** — *reporting only*; importers filed
  quarterly reports on embedded emissions, **no financial charge.** **Fact**.
- **Definitive period: from 1 Jan 2026** — financial regime: authorisation, emissions data,
  certificate purchase & surrender. **Fact** (live now).
- Implemented via a family of acts (learn the numbering — it is exam material):

| Act | Covers |
|---|---|
| IR (EU) 2023/1773 | Transitional reporting + **calculation methodology** (Annex III/IV) |
| IR (EU) 2024/3210 | **CBAM Registry** |
| IR (EU) 2025/2547 | **Calculation** in the definitive period |
| IR (EU) 2025/2546 | **Verification** |
| Del. Reg. (EU) 2025/2551 | **Accreditation** of verifiers |
| IR (EU) 2025/2621 | **Default values** |

## 1.4 Who the law aims at

CBAM places the legal duty on the **EU importer** ("the person who lodges the customs
declaration"), not on the non-EU producer. That single fact drives the whole data chain:
importers must obtain reliable emissions data from their non-EU suppliers. **Fact**.
