## Target SME profile — what "CBAM expert" must be able to do

A CBAM SME can independently:

1. Explain the regime and its relationship to the EU ETS.
2. Determine whether an import/good is **in scope** (goods, CN codes, precursors, 50 t de minimis).
3. Identify **all actors and their obligations** (declarant, operator, verifier, NCA, customs).
4. Compute **embedded emissions** — direct + indirect, actual **and** default — including
   precursors.
5. Design/assess a **monitoring plan** and system boundaries for an installation.
6. Operate the **CBAM Registry** (declarant AMM flow + operator/verifier O3CI flow).
7. Prepare **verifier-ready evidence** and understand the verification/accreditation standards.
8. Advise on **certificates, declaration, carbon-price deduction, and penalties**.
9. Apply **sector specificity** (steel BF-BOF/EAF/DRI; aluminium primary/secondary + PFC).

Courses map to these outcomes (see each course README for the mapping).

## CBAM 101 — Foundations
Goal: full conceptual command of the regime; can speak the language and navigate the workflow.
Modules: context & legal framework · scope & thresholds · actors & registry workflow ·
embedded emissions & calculation · obligations, timeline & costs · cheatsheet · self-assessment.

## CBAM 201 — Practitioner (outline)
Goal: perform and review real CBAM data work.
- Annex III/IV methodology in depth; mass-balance vs calc-based vs CEMS.
- Monitoring plans; system boundaries; data sources (ERP/MES/LIMS/utility/devices).
- Actual-vs-default decisioning; default-value catalogue (IR 2025/2621).
- Precursor chains and complex goods; sector worked examples (steel, aluminium).
- Registry operations hands-on; declaration assembly; quarterly/annual mechanics.
- Verification: ISO 14064-3 concepts, materiality, sampling; verifier-ready dossiers.

## CBAM 301 — Expert / SME (outline)
Goal: operate at verifier-grade judgment and advise at policy level.
- Accreditation: ISO 17029/14065, Del. Reg. 2025/2551, 12 accreditation activity groups.
- Verifier forensic review; risk, materiality, non-conformity handling; impartiality firewall.
- Deep sector science: steel route chemistry, aluminium PFC slope method, benchmark maths.
- Defaults methodology and where they mislead; anti-fraud/Plausibility.
- CBAM ↔ ETS ↔ DPP interplay; importer/operator liability edge cases; policy horizon.
