The Commercial Risk

If you cannot prove your true carbon footprint, EU importers will demand a discount.

Under the definitive CBAM regime, European buyers face severe financial liabilities. Without audit-proof, actual emissions data from your plant, they are legally forced to use punitive default values—and they will cut your contract prices to protect themselves.

Buyers Are De-Risking Fast

EU importers are actively dropping suppliers who can't provide flawless data sheets. Giving them a certified number protects your long-term order book.

Default Values Target the Top 10%

The EU sets default values based on the worst-polluting facilities globally. Relying on them artificially inflates your product's tax bill by 10% to 30%, erasing your cost advantage.

Generic Software Can't Handle Chemistry

Standard carbon calculators miss complex blast furnace mass balances, electrical grid mixes, and upstream precursor emissions. A single data mismatch means a rejected EU customs audit.
