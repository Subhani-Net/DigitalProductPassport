The friction

The data is at the plant, not in your buyer's spreadsheet.

Your EU importer is legally liable for the carbon embedded in your goods. Without verified actual figures, they fall back to punitive defaults — and pass the cost back to you.

Your buyer is liable

They must declare embedded emissions and surrender certificates. Every missing figure becomes their risk — and your price pressure.

Defaults punish you

Default values are deliberately conservative. Relying on them can add 10–30% to the carbon cost of your goods, and that comes back as a discount demand.

Paper isn't evidence

Bills, invoices and mill certificates are the raw material. A number without a source trail fails the verifier — and the buyer pays for the gap.

Questions

What exporters ask us.

What is CBAM, in one line?

The EU's carbon border charge. Importers of steel, aluminium and other covered goods must declare the carbon embedded in them and surrender certificates — since 1 January 2026.

My EU buyer is already handling it. Do I still need this?

They can declare only with data from your installation. Without verified actual figures they fall back to defaults, which are more expensive. That cost comes back to you as price pressure or lost orders.

Are you a verifier?

No. We produce verifier-ready evidence; the verification stamp comes from an accredited partner verifier. We say “verifier-ready, not verified” on purpose.

What data do you need?

Start with what you have: electricity and fuel bills, purchase invoices, weighbridge tickets, mill certificates (EN 10204), production logs and any supplier emissions data. No ERP access is required for the pilot.

How long does it take?

The pilot runs for two weeks. In the annual cycle, data is captured during the year and the declaration is due by 30 September of the following year — the first is 30 September 2027 for 2026 imports.

Will my buyer see my recipe or energy pricing?

No, not by default. Access is role-based, and the evidence pack is designed to share the verified figure and its evidence without exposing your process recipe or commercial energy terms.

Do you do LCA, EPD or full ESG reporting?

No. We stay narrow on CBAM evidence for steel and aluminium. We can sit alongside LCA/EPD tools and your existing sustainability reporting.

What if I don't have all the data?

We use the legal default for that line, flag it, and quantify what it costs you. Then we help you plan the data collection to replace defaults with verified actuals next cycle.
