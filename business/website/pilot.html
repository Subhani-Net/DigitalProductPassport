The pilot

A 2-week pilot on your own data.

One installation, one reporting period. You see the number, the evidence behind it, and what it saves before you commit to an annual plan.

What you give us

  • One installation and one reporting period
  • Electricity and fuel bills
  • Purchase invoices and weighbridge tickets
  • Mill certificates / batch numbers
  • Production and yield records
  • A named contact for questions

Scanned PDFs and Excel are fine. No ERP access required.

What you get back

  • Draft specific embedded emissions (SEE)
  • Evidence trail from figure to source document
  • Actual-vs-default saving in euros
  • Independent expert review of the methodology
  • Final v1 pack and a buyer walkthrough

Verifier-ready, not verified. The stamp comes from an accredited partner.

Timeline

Two weeks, four gates.

Day 0 — Kickoff (30 min)

Agree the installation, reporting period, products/CN codes and the success metric in your words.

Days 1–3 — Documents in

You send the document set. We check completeness and tell you what is missing before we start calculating.

Days 4–7 — Draft SEE + evidence

We map and validate the data, calculate the SEE, build the evidence trail and quantify the saving.

Days 8–14 — Review and v1

An independent specialist reviews the methodology. We fix, finalise v1, and walk you through it for your buyer.

Guardrails

What the pilot is — and is not.

One pilot at a time. We run a small number of pilots so each gets proper attention.
Two weeks, time-boxed. A pilot that drags becomes free consulting. We agree the scope up front.
Your data stays yours. Role-based access; your recipe and commercial energy terms are not shared with your buyer by default.
Not a verification. We produce verifier-ready evidence; the accredited verifier issues the report. We never call an advisory review “verified.”
No ERP or OT project. The pilot runs on documents you already have. Integrations are a later phase.
Fixed fee, credited. If you move to an annual plan, the pilot fee is credited to it.

How we measure success

  • A SEE you can defend, product by product.
  • Every figure traceable to a source document.
  • Actual vs default flag on every line.
  • A saving number your buyer understands.
  • An evidence pack a partner verifier is willing to review.

If the numbers are not useful, you tell us why — that feedback is part of the pilot.

Ready to see your number?

Tell us what you export and to which EU markets. We will confirm whether a pilot is a fit.
