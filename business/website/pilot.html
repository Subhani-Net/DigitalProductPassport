Risk-Free Data Diagnostic

Test your data. Uncover your pricing premium in 14 days.

Deploy your own plant records for a single production line and reporting period. See your actual carbon score, audit the evidence trail, and quantify your exact financial savings before committing to an annual compliance plan.

What You Provide (Keep Your ERP Private)

  • One defined installation boundary and historical reporting period
  • Electricity utility bills and internal fuel consumption logs
  • Upstream precursor invoices and raw weighbridge tickets
  • Mill test certificates, heat numbers, and product batch records
  • Production yield logs and mass-balance output data
  • A designated plant manager or technical lead for process mapping

Scanned PDFs, local spreadsheets, and paper records are completely fine. Absolutely no invasive ERP integrations required.

What You Receive (Your Audit-Proof Asset)

  • A defensible draft of your Specific Embedded Emissions (SEE)
  • A structured, drill-down evidence trail linking every figure to its source file
  • A clear ledger quantifying your exact tax savings in Euros vs. EU defaults
  • A rigorous boundary and methodology review backed by metallurgical engineers
  • A complete v1 compliance dossier and a strategic buyer-facing walkthrough

The Verification Boundary: Our platform prepares a verifier-ready dossier, ensuring your data is structurally flawless before your chosen accredited independent verifier steps in to provide the official stamp.

The 14-Day Blueprint

Four Milestones to Competitive Clarity.

Day 0 — Strategic Kickoff (30 min)

We align on your plant boundaries, target production routes, specific iron/steel/aluminium CN codes, and define your custom commercial success metrics.

Days 1–3 — Frictionless Document Ingestion

You securely pass your raw files to our engine. We run automated completeness checks to identify and patch data gaps before processing begins.

Days 4–7 — Metallurgical Modeling & Saving Analysis

Our process engineers map your mass-balances and precursor trails, execute the SEE carbon calculations, and lock in your true financial pricing advantage.

Days 8–14 — Engineering Review & Dossier Delivery

An industrial process specialist stress-tests the methodology against EU standards. We finalize your v1 dossier and deliver a presentation ready for your premium EU buyers.

Operational Guardrails

Clear boundaries protect your business.

Capped Cohorts. We strictly limit active pilots to ensure our metallurgical engineers provide dedicated focus to your unique production lines.
Strictly Time-Boxed. We respect your time. Scope is locked on Day 1 so we deliver actionable financial answers in exactly two weeks without project drift.
Absolute Data Sovereignty. Your proprietary recipes, energy tariffs, and supplier networks are securely ring-fenced and never broadcast to buyers by default.
A Pre-Audit, Not a Final Certificate. We engineer verifier-ready evidence. The official, independent accredited verifier issues the final stamp—ensuring a clean separation of duties.
Zero IT Overhead. The pilot operates entirely on operational papers you already hold. Complex automated software integrations are reserved for a later phase.
100% Retainer Credit. The pilot requires a predictable, transparent fixed fee. When you transition to an annual contract, 100% of this fee is credited back to your plan.

How We Measure Success Together

  • A defensive, highly competitive carbon score you can stand behind, product by product.
  • Every metric effortlessly traceable back to a physical invoice, bill, or plant meter.
  • A granular actual-vs-default breakdown across every upstream precursor material line.
