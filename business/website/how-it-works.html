The process

From paper to a verifier-ready CBAM pack.

Four moves: capture the documents, validate the data, prove the number, and reuse it across every EU buyer.

1. Capture — documents, not ERP access

Most exporters do not have a clean emissions database. They have electricity and fuel bills, purchase invoices, weighbridge tickets, mill certificates (EN 10204), production logs and Excel. That is enough to start.

We map each document to the data point it supports, with the source retained. No ERP project, no meter installation, no OT integration for the pilot.

  • Electricity and fuel bills, meter logs
  • Purchase invoices and weighbridge tickets
  • Mill certificates and batch/heat numbers
  • Production, yield and allocation records
  • Supplier precursor data, where available

2. Validate — the number has to survive a verifier

Calculators are a commodity. The product is judgement: route-specific boundaries, precursor handling, allocation, units and plausibility. We check the things an accredited verifier will check.

  • System boundary and source-stream completeness
  • Precursors for complex goods — Σ(Mi × SEEi)
  • Allocation across products and co-products
  • Units, NCV and oxidation factors
  • Plausibility against route benchmarks
  • Actual vs default determination, per line

3. Prove — an evidence pack, not a claim

Every declarable figure is traceable along a chain: declaration line → SEE → calculation → factor (source and version) → activity data → source document → row/page.

We also assemble the documentation a verifier reads first: the monitoring and evidence protocol for the installation, the assumptions, the open issues, and the determination flags.

The verifier test: pick any line. Can you, without the vendor's help, open the source document, see the factor and version, recompute the number, and see whether it is actual or default? If yes, it is dossier-grade evidence.

4. Reuse — upload once, link every buyer

The installation is the source of truth. One dataset per reporting period. Each EU importer is linked by EORI and retrieves the same verified data — no re-keying per customer, no divergent copies.

The new-buyer test: add an EU buyer tomorrow. Do you upload again, or just link EORI? With Veriflow, you link.

Precursors, without the headache

Most steel and aluminium goods are complex: they are made from inputs that are themselves CBAM goods — pig iron, crude steel, hot-rolled coil, aluminium ingot. Those inputs carry their own embedded emissions.

The rule is recursive: add the emissions of each covered precursor to the emissions of the final good.

EEinpMat = Σ (Mi × SEEi)
Mi = mass of precursor consumed · SEEi = its specific embedded emissions

Where a precursor has no verified data for the right period, the legal default applies — and we flag it. The difference between verified actual and default is often the biggest lever in the whole report.

The monitoring plan is the cover page

A monitoring plan is the installation's rule book: what is measured, how, with which instrument, how often and by whom. It is the first thing a verifier reads.

We build a forward-looking plan where it helps, and an honest evidence and methodology protocol for a year that has already elapsed. We document what was actually done — we do not backdate a plan.

The annual clock

CBAM is retrospective. You capture data during the calendar year, then declare that year by 30 September of the next. The first declaration for 2026 imports is due 30 September 2027.

Clock When What happens
Capture 1 Jan – 31 Dec Meters, invoices, supplier data, evidence.
Declare 1 Jan – 30 Sep next year Assemble, verify, declare, surrender certificates.

Who files? The EU importer files the declaration. The exporter supplies the installation data. The verifier verifies.

Where we hand over to partners

We focus entirely on the software and data trail. For everything else, we play cleanly with your existing engineers, compliance teams, and auditors.

  • Meter calibration & hardware: We do not install meters or run CEMS—we ingest the data they already produce.
  • The official audit: We do not issue the final verification stamp—we prepare the airtight digital dossier for your accredited auditor.
  • Broad ESG scopes: We focus strictly on CBAM—we leave full corporate LCAs, EPDs, and ESG reporting to your sustainability team.
  • Compliance integrity: We document what was actually done—we never invent retrospective monitoring plans.
