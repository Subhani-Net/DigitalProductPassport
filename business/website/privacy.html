Privacy notice

Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Working draft for review

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Veriflow (working name), based in London, United Kingdom. For the purposes of data protection law, we are the controller of the personal data described below.

Contact: hello@veriflow.example (placeholder — replace before launch).

2. What we collect

When you submit the contact form, we collect the information you choose to provide: your name, company, role, email address, country, products/CN codes, EU markets and message. We also record the time of submission and whether you gave consent.

Our hosting and form infrastructure may process basic technical data (such as the page requested and standard server logs) to operate and secure the site.

3. Why we use it

  • To respond to your enquiry and discuss whether a pilot is suitable.
  • To keep a record of the enquiry and any follow-up.
  • To operate, secure and improve the website.

4. Legal basis

We rely on our legitimate interests in responding to business enquiries and operating the website, and on your consent where the law requires it (for example, for any optional communications). You can withdraw consent at any time.

5. Who we share it with

We use service providers to run this site and store enquiries, including:

  • Microsoft Azure — hosting (Azure Static Web Apps) and storage (Azure Table Storage).
  • GitHub — source code hosting and deployment.

We do not sell your personal data. We may disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights.

6. International transfers

Our providers may process data in countries outside the UK/EEA. Where that happens, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Addendum or the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. How long we keep it

We keep enquiry records for up to 24 months from the last contact, unless a longer period is needed for a live engagement or required by law. After that, we delete or anonymise them.

8. Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising or analytics cookies at launch. If we add privacy-friendly analytics later, we will update this notice.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data portability. To exercise a right, contact us at the address above.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), or your local EU authority.

10. Changes

We may update this notice from time to time. The date at the top shows the latest version.

Note: this is a working draft, not legal advice. Have it reviewed before launch.
