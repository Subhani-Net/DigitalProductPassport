# Dossier — CBAM Verification Bodies (Arena C)

The accredited-verifier landscape that gates Art. 8 CBAM (definitive phase). Threat = LOW
(we interoperate/partner, never compete); they define the verification bar our C5 outputs
must pass.

| Body | CBAM status (Sep 2026) | Named accreditations | DPP services | Note for us |
|------|------------------------|----------------------|--------------|-------------|
| TÜV SÜD | Verification + pre-verification + training | DAkkS (DE), ACCREDIA (IT), **NABCB (India)**, CNAS (CN), SINGLAS (SG) + ISO 14065; process ISO 14064-3 | ESPR portfolio incl. EU battery passport, seminars | **Strongest partner for India supplier-side verification** |
| TÜV Rheinland | Verification + pre-verification + reporting/certificate support | "accredited verification body" (AB not named on page) | not found | Partner option; Certipedia registry |
| SGS | **Pre-verification + training only** — "intends to apply for ISO/IEC 17029 extension to include CBAM… subject to assessment" | EU ETS-accredited verifier (several states) | not found | Not yet able to verify CBAM definitive-phase data |
| Bureau Veritas | Verification + pre-verification + advisory; "**applicant** for CBAM verifier accreditation" | "proven competence under ISO 17029 and 14065" | battery passport w/ Circulor | Applicant — verification capacity scarce |
| DNV | CBAM **advisory only** (no Art. 8 verification offered on its page) | not stated on CBAM page | DPP services: creation, system interoperability, "validate and verify DPP data integrity" | Advisory + DPP-data partner |
| Intertek | "end-to-end" CBAM support "with our CBAM experts and verifiers"; supplier data collection, embedded-emissions calc, pre-verification, reporting | ISO 14064-3 for GHG verification; AA1000/ISAE3000 | DPP Advisory & Managed Services; textile DPP | Broad but accreditation depth unstated |

**Implication (compliance-matrix §3):** scarce accredited capacity through 2026–27 ⇒
"verifier-ready data" is a sellable property, and a TÜV-SÜD-type partner covers the
India side of our two-sided wedge. CleanCarbon's in-house 14065/17029 certs look weak
against this benchmark (certificate ≠ listed CBAM verifier).
