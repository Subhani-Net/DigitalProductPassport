# Dossier — Sentra.World / "Sentra Calculus" (Arena A, MEDIUM threat)

- **What:** India-based carbon-accounting platform. EEPC India CBAM-panel member (listed as
  Sentra.World Technologies Pvt Ltd, contact Harsh Choudhry, Co-Founder & CEO). Product
  marketed as a carbon-management suite ("Sentra Calculus").
- **Claims (vendor):** precise calculations for **CCF, PCF, LCA and EPD** across **Scope
  1/2/3**; configurable with **200+ manufacturing processes**; **25,000+ emission factors**;
  **150+ plants modelled across the steel value chain — mining to end products**. **Claim.**
- **What that means:** a broad carbon-accounting / LCA-EPD platform with a process library,
  factor database and steel-specific plant archetypes. It can generate many voluntary report
  types (corporate footprint, product footprint, LCA, EPD).
- **CBAM depth:** EEPC-listed as a CBAM advisory provider; steel/aluminium exporter reach. But
  the public copy does **not** claim CBAM-specific evidence dossiers, verifier-grade audit
  trails, EORI reuse or a verification stamp. **NOT FOUND** in the supplied copy.
- **Verification signal:** none claimed in the supplied copy. (Sentra is a consultant/platform,
  not an accredited verifier.)
- **DPP / registry:** none claimed in the supplied copy. **NOT FOUND.**
- **Threat read (vs our rubric):** **MEDIUM.**
  - Wedge adjacency: **yes** (India steel/aluminium exporters, EEPC panel).
  - Supplier-side reach: **yes** (exporter-facing; LCA/EPD breadth).
  - DPP sell-twice bridge: **not claimed**.
  - Evidence depth: **not claimed** (breadth is accounting, not CBAM validation).
- **Our differentiation vs them:** validation intelligence, audit room, verifier-ready dossier,
  exporter→importer reuse via EORI, zero-exposure target, partner-only stamp. They sell *the
  number*; we sell *the number a verifier will accept*.
- **Complementarity (not a partnership):** their LCA/EPD capability could serve a customer's
  broader needs; we stay the CBAM evidence layer. **Posture: competitor only** (user decision,
  Nov 2026) — do not engage as advisor or partner.
- **Watch items:** (1) any CBAM evidence/registry-output launch; (2) any "verified" claim;
  (3) pricing for SME exporters; (4) whether they encode validation rules (the moat risk);
  (5) EEPC panel activity that reaches our target exporter list first.
- **Caveats:** claims are vendor statements; "modelled" ≠ audited clients; factor libraries are
  often licensed and version-sensitive — verify before treating as a moat.
