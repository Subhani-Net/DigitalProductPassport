# Expert Bench — longlist & pipeline

Tracks the CBAM verifier-grade advisors we approach. Companion to `expert-brief.md`.
**Goal:** 2–3 named advisors; **one paid 3-hr review on the customer's draft v0 (synthetic twin)
before external delivery**; formal readiness validation after C2–C4.
**Accepted (Nov 2026):** India + EU mix; ~£500 for the first 1–2 reviews.

**Status:** `Target` → `Approached` → `Screening` → `Reviewed` (paid micro-review done) →
`Advisor` (ongoing) · `Parked` · `No`.
**Type:** `SME` (advisory) · `ExV` (ex-verifier) · `Acad` (academic) · `Body` (trade body).

> Named individuals are **blank on purpose** — fill from LinkedIn/directories/the call. Never invent names.
> **Independence rule:** an advisor must not verify the same client's declaration (ISO 17029/14065).

## Approach messages

**LinkedIn note (≤300 chars):**
> "Hi [Name] — I'm building a supplier-side CBAM engine for steel/aluminium (UK, pre-revenue). I'd value **3 paid hours** to stress-test our SEE methodology against ISO 14064-3. May I send a short brief?"

**Follow-up:**
> "Thanks for connecting. We turn plant bills/invoices into a verifier-ready SEE. Before the first customer report, I want an expert to poke holes in it — **3 hours, paid, written critique**, on a synthetic docket. 15-min call first if useful?"

**Subject:** *Paid 3-hr review: CBAM SEE methodology (steel)*

Lead with the **artifact, not a pitch** — offer the synthetic docket (no NDA needed to read a synthetic case).

## Screening call (15 min, free)

1. Have you worked on CBAM SEE or steel/Al PCF?
2. Do you know Reg. 2025/2547 and ISO 14064-3?
3. Will you name concrete boundary/allocation/precursor problems?
4. Any conflict with an accredited verification body?
5. Available in the next two weeks?

## Screening checklist (before the paid review)

- [ ] Steel **or** aluminium process depth (not generic ESG).
- [ ] Knows 2023/956 + 2025/2547 (and 2025/2551 for verification).
- [ ] Can read a pilot docket and name concrete red flags.
- [ ] No conflict with an accredited body stamping the same clients.
- [ ] Willing to sign NDA + IP assignment.
- [ ] India-based (timezone/clusters) or EU-based (regulation) — one of each.
