# Priority Leads — Contact-Ready Call Sheet (India exporters)

Turns the 51-lead directory (`leads-india-exporters.md`) into a **call sequence**. Status lives in
`tracker.md`; this file is the "who to call next and why" layer. Contacts are **not verified** —
use the website contact page, LinkedIn company page, or IndiaMART seller page, and ask for the
**owner / export head**. Never invent an email or phone number.

## How to work it

1. **10 calls a week** from Tier 1, then Tier 2.
2. **Call first.** Get the export/owner name; then send the one-pager on WhatsApp/email.
3. **Log the same day** in `tracker.md` (stage, last touch, next action, next date).
4. **One at a time** — no mass email. Personalise the angle by segment.
5. Boundary in every conversation: **"verifier-ready, not verified."**

## Guardrails

- **No fabricated contacts.** Use the site contact page / LinkedIn / IndiaMART; ask for the
  owner or export head.
- **Call-first**, then WhatsApp/email the one-pager (send within minutes of the call).
- **Don't promise “verified.”** Say: *"verifier-ready; the stamp comes from an accredited partner."*
- **Log the same day** in `tracker.md` — every row needs a next action and a next date.
- **If the gatekeeper asks why:** "It's about your EU shipments and the carbon data your buyer is
  now required to declare."
