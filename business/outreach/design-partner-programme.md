# Design-Partner Programme (Free Pilot)

**Goal:** 2–3 **free design partners** who give us real data, feedback and a reference — while we
prove the method before charging. Free is deliberate, but **not free consulting**: the exchange is
explicit and time-boxed.

## 1. Who we want

An Indian SME exporter of steel/aluminium goods with:
- EU buyers (current or intended),
- data in bills/invoices/mill certificates/weighbridge/Excel,
- a decision-maker reachable by phone,
- willingness to give 30 minutes of feedback and (optionally) a reference.

## 2. The offer

- **Free 2-week pilot**, one installation, one reporting period.
- Draft SEE per product/CN code, an **evidence trail**, and the **actual-vs-default saving**.
- A **diagnostic report** (our template) and a walkthrough.
- No fee; no obligation to buy.

## 3. The exchange (non-negotiable)

| They give | We give |
|---|---|
| Real (even S0/S1) data | The draft SEE + evidence + saving on their numbers |
| A named champion + 30 min feedback | Fast turnaround and hand-holding |
| Permission to reference (named or anonymous) | Early access and a preferred price on the first paid year |
| Constructive criticism | Fixes that become product |

**No obligation to buy.** If it works, we discuss the annual plan; if not, we ask for a clear “no”
and the reason — that is still valuable.

## 4. Guardrails

- **2–3 partners max**, one pilot at a time.
- **Two weeks**, then a decision.
- Scope = one installation, one period, agreed CN codes.
- **No free consulting** beyond the pilot scope.
- Data confidentiality; no client data in the synthetic dossier; no buyer exposure.
- **“Verifier-ready, not verified.”** No review/verifier claims until genuinely earned.

## 6. Outreach sequence

1. **Call** the published number; ask for the owner/export head.
2. **Offer the free pilot** explicitly: one installation, two weeks, no fee, no obligation.
3. Send the one-pager + data request checklist (same day).
4. Book a 15-minute kickoff.
5. Log in `tracker.md` — stage `Pilot`, note **“Design partner (free)”**.

## 7. Timeline

| Week | Action |
|---|---|
| 1 | 10 calls; 3–4 conversations; select 2–3 partners |
| 2 | Kickoff + documents; mapping and draft |
| 3 | Draft SEE + evidence; review; v1 |
| 4 | Feedback, reference request, conversion conversation |

## 8. Metrics

- 10 approached → 4 conversations → **2–3 pilots** → 1–2 completed → **1 reference** → **1 paid**.
- Track time-to-first-document and time-to-draft.

## 9. What we will not claim

- No “reviewed by independent specialists” until the review is actually done.
- No “verified”, “certified”, “accredited”, “audit-guaranteed”.
- No client logos or testimonials without written permission.
