# Customer Funnel (exporters, India)

How we turn a broad universe of CBAM-scope Indian SME exporters into pilots and paying customers.
Runs in parallel with the investor rail; solution-led, not probing.

## Stages

| # | Stage | Definition | Exit criterion |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Universe** | Indian SMEs exporting steel/aluminium/fastener/forging goods to the EU | in CBAM scope |
| 2 | **Lead** | Named company with public presence + a why-fit | added to `leads-india-exporters.md` |
| 3 | **Contacted** | Solution-led email/LinkedIn sent | outreach sent |
| 4 | **Engaged** | Reply / call booked | 10-min walkthrough held |
| 5 | **Qualified** | Exports to EU + CBAM pain + decision-maker | discovery call done, pain confirmed |
| 6 | **Pilot** | Signed 2-week concierge pilot (paid or LOI) | data received, report delivered |
| 7 | **Paid** | Converts to Base plan (€8–15k/yr) | contract + first invoice |
| 8 | **Advocate** | Case study + referrals | reference + intros |

## Funnel maths (solo, part-time — 90-day plan)

| Metric | Target (90 days) |
|---|---|
| Leads identified | 50 |
| Contacted | 30 |
| Engaged (reply/call) | 6–10 (20–30%) |
| Qualified | 3–5 |
| Pilots | 1–2 |
| Paid | 1 |
| Advocate | 1 |

Rule of thumb: **~30 leads → ~2 pilots → ~1 paid** in the first cycle. Volume on the top, patience on the bottom.

## Channels

- **EEPC India / FIEO** member directories (engineering / metal exporters).
- **IndiaMART / TradeIndia** supplier listings (by product + city).
- **Direct web search** (this lead list) + company contact forms / LinkedIn.
- **Partner verifiers / consultants** (the Xero model — they carry their client base).
- **Referrals** from the first pilot.

## Cadence

- **Weekly:** add 10 leads, send 10 first-touches, follow up.
- **Log every signal** (per lead) → feeds the investor story.
- **One pilot at a time** (concierge capacity), then automate.

## Signal rubric (per contact)

- **Strong:** asks price / asks to try / introduces a colleague / shares real data.
- **Medium:** detailed questions, compares to current tool.
- **Weak:** polite interest, no follow-up.
