# Expert Brief — CBAM Verifier-Grade Advisors (ad-hoc bench)

One-page brief to send to potential advisors. Purpose: **buy domain knowledge to encode into the
product**, not the verification stamp. Honest about stage; small, paid, time-boxed.

## What we are (honest stage)

A UK-based pre-revenue startup building the **supplier-side CBAM engine** for steel & aluminium:
turns a plant's existing documents (bills, invoices, tickets, mill certificates) into a
**verifier-ready** embodied-emissions report (SEE) that EU importers can reuse via EORI. We are
running **concierge pilots** while the software is built. We are **not** a verifier and will never
issue the stamp.

| 1 | Stress-test our SEE methodology on a real pilot docket | written critique + severity list |
| 2 | Stress-test our data model / evidence dossier against ISO 14064-3 | red-flag list a verifier would raise |

## The review pack (what we send)

1. **Synthetic twin of the real report** (structure + plausible numbers; no client-identifying data).
2. **Evidence trail** (which input → which factor → which figure).
3. **Calc assumptions** + the source-document types used.
4. **One-page "what we want challenged"** memo with the six questions below.

## Review scope

| In scope | Out of scope |
|---|---|
| SEE methodology on the pack | legal opinion |
| Data model / evidence vs ISO 14064-3 | accreditation advice |
| Report/format gaps | any "verified" statement |

**Deliverable:** written critique with severity + rule/check/test-case per finding
(`knowledge/research/expert-session-template.md`). **Fee:** fixed, paid on delivery.

## The six stress-test questions

1. **System boundary & precursors** — does our scope capture every step per Reg. (EU) 2025/2547
   (coke/sinter, BF/BOF/EAF, casting, rolling, on-site power, BFG/BOF gas; alumina/anode/electrolysis)?
2. **Allocation** — mass vs energy vs economic for multi-product plants; co-products (slag, off-gases, heat).
3. **Electricity / indirect** — grid vs PPA, India grid factor, imported/exported electricity.
4. **Defaults vs actuals** — when are actuals admissible; is our "saving vs default" defensible?
5. **Evidence & materiality (ISO 14064-3)** — what makes our dossier verifier-ready; sampling plan.
6. **Registry / format** — does our output map to the CBAM declaration and verification-report structure?
