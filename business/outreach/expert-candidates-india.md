## Screening rubric (score 1–5 each; threshold ≥20/30)

| Dimension | 1 | 5 |
|---|---|---|
| Steel/Al process depth | generic ESG | route-specific (BF-BOF/EAF/DRI/Al) |
| CBAM methodology | awareness | has prepared/reviewed SEE, precursors, allocation |
| ISO 14064-3 / verification | heard of it | certified auditor/verifier or accredited-body experience |
| India context | none | grid factor, clusters, supplier data reality |
| Availability | none | can commit 3–6 paid hours/month |
| Commercial fit | high day rate only | reasonable ad-hoc rate |
| Conflict | competitor/accredited body | independent/clean |

## Screening questions

1. Have you prepared or reviewed a CBAM SEE for steel or aluminium? Which route and CN codes?
2. How do you handle precursors, allocation and waste gases?
3. What is your ISO 14064-3 / ISO 14065 / 17029 experience? Which body?
4. Have you worked with Indian plants/grid factors? Which clusters?
5. Can you commit 3–6 paid hours a month on an ad-hoc basis?
6. Any conflict with a CBAM software vendor or an accredited verifier?
7. What is your day rate?
8. Are you comfortable with NDA + IP assignment and an independence declaration?
