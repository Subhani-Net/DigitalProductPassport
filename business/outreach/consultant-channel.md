# Consultant Channel & Sourcing

Consultants are **both** a knowledge source (verifier-grade domain) **and** a distribution
channel (the "accountants/bookkeepers" of the Xero model). Locked: engage the verifier-grade
consultant **post C2–C4 scaffolding** (to detail schema/data-capture/calculation models).

## Sourcing channels
- **Leafr** (marketplace, ~48h matching) — `https://www.leafr.com/job-roles/cbam-consultant`.
  Their specialist profiles show the exact expertise we need (steel PCF, CBAM calc).
- Direct: TÜV SÜD (India reach via NABCB), SGS, Bureau Veritas, DNV, Intertek — as *partners*.
- Referrals from the exporter pilots themselves.

## Sequencing (do NOT engage yet)
1. Build the **C2–C4 scaffolding** (canonical model → ingestion → harmonization) first.
2. Then engage **2–3 consultants** for **readiness validation** (does our data model + calc
   survive their scrutiny) — this is *co-development*, not a hire-to-scale.
3. Then convert 1–2 into a **partner program** (resell / revenue-share, Xero model).

## The offer — what to propose (and what NOT to)

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **Free early access + co-development** for validation ("help us stress-test the data model") | ❌ Pay them upfront to "use" the software |
| **Revenue-share partner program** later (they resell, take a cut) | ❌ Buy usage — distorts signal, burns cash |
| **Private design-partner program**, honest about stage | ❌ Stealth-brand into dishonesty |

**Why not pay them to use it:** (1) it's fake traction; (2) it's expensive pre-raise; (3) they
optimise for the payment, not the product. **Co-development + revenue-share** keeps the signal
honest and costs nothing up front.

## Role clarity (do not conflate)
- **CBAM consultant** = advisory/domain knowledge (what we buy post-C2–C4).
- **Accredited verifier** = the *stamp* (partner, never us — independence rule).
- **Channel partner** = distribution (they bring client engagements through our software).
