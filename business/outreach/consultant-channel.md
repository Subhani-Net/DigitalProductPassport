# Consultant / Expert Channel & Sourcing

Advisors are **both** a knowledge source (verifier-grade domain) **and** a distribution channel
(the "accountants/bookkeepers" of the Xero model).

**Amended Nov 2026 (accepted):** network now — do not wait for C2–C4. Run **one paid 2–4 hour
micro-review before the first pilot report** to de-risk the methodology; do the formal readiness
validation **after** C2–C4. Mix: **one India-based + one EU-based** advisor. Budget ≈ **£500** for
the first 1–2 reviews.

Supporting files: `expert-brief.md` (what we send), `expert-bench.md` (longlist + pipeline +

## Sequencing (amended)

| When | What | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Now (parallel with customer calls)** | Longlist 20–30; approach 8–10; book the paid slot | relationships + capacity |
| **First customer's data → internal draft v0** | Build the report; label it **"draft, pending expert review"** | real artifact first |
| **Before external delivery** | **1 paid 3-hr review** on the report's **synthetic twin** | higher-signal critique on a real deliverable |
| **Before external delivery** | Fix **v1** from findings; deliver v1, never v0 | protect credibility |
| **After C2–C4** | 2–3 advisors, 2–3 days each: formal readiness validation of schema / calc / evidence | validate the locked model |
| **Later** | Convert 1–2 to design partner / advisory retainer; then revenue-share channel | Xero model |

## Data handling (customer-first review)

1. Customer shares S0/S1 data under the pilot agreement.
2. We build **draft v0** internally — marked **"draft, pending expert review"**; never sent as final.
3. We build a **synthetic twin** (structure + plausible numbers; strip company name, EORI, exact
   volumes/prices, supplier names, recipes, energy contracts, location).
4. Expert signs NDA; receives the twin + evidence trail + the six questions; 3 hrs; written critique.
5. Findings → rules/checks/test cases → **v1 delivered to the customer**.
6. Never cite an accredited body's mark or call it "verified".

## The offer — what to propose (and what NOT to)

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| **Paid micro-consultation** for time + judgment (2–4 hrs, written critique) | ❌ Pay them to "use" the software (fake traction, distorts signal) |
| **Free early access + co-development** for validation ("stress-test the data model") | ❌ Stealth-brand into dishonesty |
| **Revenue-share partner program** later (they resell, take a cut) | ❌ Let them become the stamp |

**Why not pay them to use it:** (1) fake traction; (2) expensive pre-raise; (3) they optimise for
the payment, not the product. **Advisory hours are different** — paying fairly for expertise is
expected and keeps the signal honest.

## Role clarity (do not conflate)

- **Advisory SME** = domain knowledge we encode into the product (paid ad-hoc).
- **Accredited verifier** = the *stamp* (partner, never us — independence rule ISO 17029/14065).
- **Channel partner** = distribution (they bring client engagements through our software).

## Terms

**"verifier-ready, not verified."** Every session must produce rules, checks and test cases
(`expert-session-template.md`) — judgment becomes product IP, not a memory that walks out the door.
