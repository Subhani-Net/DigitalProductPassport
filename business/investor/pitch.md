# Pitch

The story, at three lengths. Consistent with `investor-deck.md` and the deep docs.

## Elevator (30 seconds)

The EU's carbon border tax is live, and every EU importer of steel and aluminium must prove the
carbon in their goods — or pay punitive default values. The data sits with non-EU suppliers, who
are unserved and drowning in paper. We turn a supplier's messy data into a clean, verifier-ready,
zero-exposure upload — once, reused by every EU buyer — so the importer survives audit at the
lowest cost and the supplier keeps its orders. We start with the CBAM wedge that's in force now,
and the same data becomes their Digital Product Passport later.

## Narrative (3 minutes)

1. **The regime.** CBAM went definitive on 1 Jan 2026; first certificate surrender is 30 Sep 2027.
   Importers are liable; exporters hold the data; verified actuals beat punitive defaults.
2. **The gap.** SAP serves declarants, not suppliers. CarbonChain has no DPP and closed evidence.
   Verification capacity is scarce. The non-EU supplier side — India, Turkey, UK, China — is
   the unserved wedge.
3. **The product.** Four friction pillars on a commodity calculator: **Capture** (messy data in),
   **Validation intelligence** (survives the verifier), **Audit room** (evidence + drill-down),
   **Trust** (upload-once, zero-exposure). We don't sell a calculator; we sell verifiable data
   with minimal friction.
4. **The moat.** Encoded validation intelligence — every verifier finding and new route makes the
   rule library stronger. It compounds; generic tools can't catch up.
5. **The model.** Two-sided SaaS + verifier channel, €8–15k base ACV. Sell the CBAM wedge now,
   reuse the same data for the DPP later (sell-twice).
6. **The ask.** ~£1.0–1.25M (SEIS + EIS) to ship the engine and win the first 30+ customers,
   with a deferred Series A gated on traction.

## For a customer (exporter, solution-led — not probing)

"I've built a working CBAM pipeline for steel. Most exporters struggle to produce verifier-ready
actuals, so importers fall back to costly defaults. Want a 10-minute walkthrough on a sample —
and if useful, a pilot on your data?"

## For a verifier

"Your audits take hours on PDFs and emails. We give you a read-only evidence room with drill-down
from any figure to its source document — recalculate, verify, issue the report faster."
