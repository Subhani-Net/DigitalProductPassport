# Business Plan

Consolidated view for investors. Deep detail lives in the referenced docs; this is the
single-page narrative.

## 1. Problem
CBAM is live (definitive 1 Jan 2026; first surrender 30 Sep 2027). EU importers of steel &
aluminium are liable for embedded emissions; without verified actuals they pay punitive
defaults. The data sits with non-EU suppliers, who are unserved (SAP is declarant-only) and
operate on paper/Excel.

## 2. Solution
A two-sided SaaS that turns a supplier's messy data into a **verifiable, registrable, defensible
declaration** — four friction pillars (Capture / Validation / Audit / Trust) on a commodity
calculator. *Beyond processing:* Extract · Judge · Prove · Coordinate · Advise · Complete.

## 3. Market
6,000–12,000 EU metals declarants; 20,000–50,000 non-EU exporters; €75.36/t certificate price.
Legally compelled spend.

## 4. Competitive position
Supplier-side + verifier-ready + zero-exposure + metals-DPP bridge. Moat = accumulated validation
intelligence. (`../competition/friction-gap-analysis.md`)

## 5. Business model
Two-sided SaaS (exporter + importer) + verifier as third class + Xero-partner channel.
Pricing L €1–3k / B €8–15k / H €30–60k.

## 6. Financials
Base ARR €0.3–0.45M (2028) → €1.2–1.8M; blue-sky £10M+. Raise ~£1.0–1.25M (SEIS + EIS),
Series A deferred on traction. (`investment-plan.md`, `../research/goto-market-sizing.md`)

## 7. Roadmap
M1 done → M2 (CBAM engine, first revenue) → M3 (sell-twice) → steel GTM (2027–28) →
textiles (2029). (`../product-roadmap.md` §5)

## 8. Risks
Regulatory drift (schemas-as-data) · competition (wedge on their gaps) · validation (only via
pilot) · fragility (spec-first + tests). (`../product-roadmap.md` §9)
