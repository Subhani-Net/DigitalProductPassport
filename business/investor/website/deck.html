What is CBAM

A border tax on the carbon embedded in imports.

CBAM (Regulation (EU) 2023/956) prices the carbon in goods imported into the EU, matching the cost EU producers pay under the ETS — to stop "carbon leakage". Covers cement, iron & steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity, hydrogen — by customs (CN) code, including the precursors used to make them.

The reporting phase is over — the financial phase went live 1 Jan 2026.

The data problem

Who pays ≠ who has the data.

Importers face the fines; the non-EU factory holds the emissions data — in fragmented, unstructured form: mill certificates, coal/utility invoices, hand-kept weighbridge logs.

SAP and legacy platforms build for the declarant (EU buyer). The supplier-side data-extraction layer is unserved.

The calculation problem

Why generic calculators fail.

Route specificity

Blast furnace ≈ 2.1, electric arc ≈ 0.45, DRI, aluminium ≈ 16.5 tCO₂/t.

Precursor roll-ups

Embedded carbon of upstream covered inputs.

Methodology rigour

Mass-balance vs CEMS vs calculation-based — CBAM allocation, not the GHG Protocol.

If the numbers fail an accredited verifier's audit, the value is zero. A calculator calculates; a workflow system proves.
