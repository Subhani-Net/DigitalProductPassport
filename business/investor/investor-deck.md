## Slide 1 — Title

**A CBAM data platform for metals** — turn a messy supplier's data into a verifiable,
registrable, defensible declaration, once, reused by every EU buyer.

*Exporter-first · in-force wedge now · DPP later.*

## Slide 2 — The problem

- **CBAM is live and mandatory** (definitive phase since 1 Jan 2026; first certificate
  surrender **30 Sep 2027**).
- **Defaults are punitive** (~10% mark-up in 2026 → ~30% by 2028) — without verified actuals,
  importers overpay and exporters lose orders.
- **The data doesn't exist in clean form** — mills run on paper, PDFs, Excel; SAP is
  declarant-only, so the **non-EU supplier side is unserved**.
- **Verification is scarce** (SGS/BV still applicants) — "verifier-ready data" is the bottleneck.

A **legally compelled** spend, not a speculative market.

## Slide 4 — The wedge

**Sell the compliance need that is in force NOW; deliver DPP when its act lands.**

- CBAM (live) → revenue from day one; the same verified-data pipeline is reused for the
  **steel/aluminium DPP** (~2028–29) — the *sell-twice* engine.
- Regulatory drift is neutralised: schemas-as-data, anchored on the in-force wedge.

## Slide 5 — The product: four friction pillars (not a calculator)

The calculator/connectivity is commodity. The product is the friction around it:

1. **Capture** — messy data in (adapters, auto-map, unit auto-detect).
2. **Validation intelligence** — numbers that *survive an accredited verifier*.
3. **Audit room** — evidence provenance + verifier drill-down.
4. **Trust & hand-off** — upload-once, EORI link, zero-exposure.

*Beyond processing*: Extract · Judge · Prove · Coordinate · Advise · Complete
(`../jobs-to-be-done.md`).

## Slide 6 — Why us / the moat

- **Encoded validation intelligence** — metallurgy/route/verifier judgment accumulated as a rule
  library; compounds with every verifier finding and every new route (generic calculators can't
  catch up).
- **Supplier-side + verifier-ready evidence** — the gap SAP leaves open.
- **Zero-exposure trust** — proof without revealing the supplier's recipe/energy mix.
- **Metals DPP bridge** — the sell-twice conversion.

## Slide 9 — Business model

- **Two-sided SaaS** (exporter + importer) + **verifier as third user class**.
- **Xero-model channel**: verifiers/consultants/customs agents sell on the platform.
- **Pricing:** Low €1–3k · Base €8–15k · High €30–60k · services line.
