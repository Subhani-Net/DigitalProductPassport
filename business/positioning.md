# Positioning — The CBAM Evidence Layer

**One-liner:** *We turn an exporter's existing documents into a verifier-ready CBAM evidence
pack — one that every EU buyer can reuse — without exposing the plant's recipe.*

This note keeps sales honest and sharp. It does not change the product roadmap.

---

## 1. Who we are — and who we are not

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| The **CBAM evidence + validation layer** for exporters | A full carbon-accounting suite |
| A producer of **verifier-ready** dossiers | A verifier (we never issue the stamp) |
| Focused on **steel & aluminium** (then textiles) | An LCA/EPD/CCF platform |
| A **document → SEE → evidence** pipeline | An ERP/PLM system of record |
| Built for **reuse across EU buyers via EORI** | A one-off consulting report |

**Boundary line:** *"verifier-ready, not verified."* Say it early; it builds trust.

---

## 2. The job we own (customer's words)

> "My EU buyer is asking for carbon data. I have bills, invoices, tickets and mill
> certificates — not an LCA team. I need a number my buyer can use, that survives their
> verifier, without handing over my recipe or energy pricing."

We own: **capture → validate → prove → reuse**. Not: corporate footprint, LCA, EPD authoring.

## 6. Objection handling

**"Sentra/others do LCA, EPD, CCF — a full suite."**
> "They're strong on broad carbon accounting. We're deliberately narrow: the CBAM evidence your
> EU buyer's verifier will accept, from documents you already have. If you need LCA/EPD, we work
> alongside those tools — but the CBAM declaration is a different, regulated job."

**"Why not just use a consultant?"**
> "You can. The difference is reuse and evidence: we produce a traceable pack you can reuse
> across buyers, not a one-off report. And we're cheaper than a consultant's hourly work."

**"Are you verified / accredited?"**
> "We're verifier-ready, not verified. The stamp comes from an accredited partner — that's the
> independence rule. We make their job fast because every figure traces to a source."

**"Do you do corporate footprint / BRSR / CSRD?"**
> "No — that's a different buyer and a different job. We stay on CBAM so we can be excellent at
> it. For broader reporting we'd point you to a platform."

**"How do I know your number is right?"**
> "Every figure drills down: document → factor → calculation. An independent expert reviews the
> methodology before the first customer report. You see the saving against the default."

## 7. Proof we must build (in order)

1. One pilot end-to-end: v0 → expert review → v1 accepted by the customer.
2. One evidence dossier that survives expert scrutiny.
3. Registry-output parity (XML/Excel) — so "declaration-ready" is true.
4. One named partner-verifier path.
5. One case-study line (customer reference) — the trust asset.

Until (1)–(3) exist, keep the promise to "pilot," not "platform."

---

## 8. Sales boundary (locked)

- **Exporter-first**; importers opportunistic; verifiers as partners.
- **Steel & aluminium** first; textiles later.
- **Partner-only stamp**; never self-verify.
- **Narrow wedge**; never claim full-suite carbon accounting.
