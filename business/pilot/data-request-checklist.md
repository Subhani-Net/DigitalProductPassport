## Privacy and submission

- Files are stored in a restricted environment accessible only to the pilot lead and the
  independent reviewer.
- Your **recipes, chemical allocations and commercial energy tariffs** are not shared with buyers.
- High-resolution scanned PDFs, spreadsheets, and clear images are acceptable.
- **No ERP or live database access is needed** for this diagnostic.
- Please name files as `[YYYY-MM]_[TYPE]_[short-description].[ext]`
  (e.g. `2026-01_ELECTRICITY_jan-bill.pdf`).

## Minimum viable set (start here)

If you cannot provide everything, send these first:
1. Plant process flow / layout (even a photo of a whiteboard diagram).
2. 12 months of electricity bills (MWh).
3. Fuel purchase invoices (coal, gas, diesel, fuel oil).
4. Production output by product/CN code.
5. Purchase invoices + mill certificates for primary metal inputs.

---

## 1. Facility & Boundary Mapping

| # | Item | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1.1 | Plant layout or process flow diagram | PDF / image | Defines system boundaries and process steps |
| 1.2 | Production outputs (tonnes) by product and EU CN code | spreadsheet / PDF | The activity level for each good |
| 1.3 | Production route (BF-BOF / EAF / DRI; primary / secondary aluminium) | note | Route-specific calculation and benchmarks |

## 2. Direct Fuel & Energy (Scope 1)

| # | Item | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 2.1 | Fuel purchase invoices / delivery logs (coal, natural gas, diesel, fuel oil) | PDF / spreadsheet | Activity data for direct combustion |
| 2.2 | Coke / reducing-agent records (BF, DRI) | PDF / spreadsheet | Process emissions and reduction mass balance |
| 2.3 | On-site fuel consumption logs, if separate from invoices | spreadsheet | Cross-check quantities |

## 3. Electricity & Utilities (Scope 2)

| # | Item | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 3.1 | Monthly grid electricity bills showing total MWh | PDF | Purchased electricity |
| 3.2 | Captive power plant fuel logs (if applicable) | spreadsheet | On-site generation emissions |
| 3.3 | PPA / Guarantees of Origin evidence (if claiming a lower factor) | PDF | Contractual electricity claims |
| 3.4 | Imported/exported steam or heat records (if any) | spreadsheet | Boundary completeness |

## 4. Upstream Precursor Inflow (the supply-chain link)

| # | Item | Format | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 4.1 | Purchase invoices for primary metal inputs (billets, scrap, DRI, alumina, unwrought aluminium) | PDF / spreadsheet | Precursor mass and supplier |
| 4.2 | Mill certificates / supplier data sheets | PDF | Precursor SEE where available |
| 4.3 | Weighbridge tickets / aggregated mass logs | PDF / spreadsheet | Exact precursor mass entering the gate |
| 4.4 | Supplier verification reports (if any) | PDF | Actual precursor data is admissible only with a valid report for the right period |

## What we do NOT need

- ERP/database credentials
- Your customer list or contract prices
- Your production recipe beyond the process flow needed for boundaries
- Personal data unrelated to the pilot contact

## If a document is missing

We will use the **legal default** for that line, flag it clearly, and quantify the cost. You can
replace defaults with actuals in a later cycle.
