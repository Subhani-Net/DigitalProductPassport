| Input | Mass in (t) | Output | Mass out (t) | Yield % | Plausible? |

- If **inputs ≫ outputs**, check for stock changes, scrap, or missing outputs.
- If **outputs > inputs**, check units, period, or unrecorded inputs.
- Record by-products (slag, scale, trim) — they affect allocation/mass balance.
