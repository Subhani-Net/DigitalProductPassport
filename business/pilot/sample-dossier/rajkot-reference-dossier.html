Boundary basis

Direct process emissions are counted from on-site combustion (natural gas, LDO) and electrode consumption. Purchased billets and wire rod are CBAM-covered inputs, so their upstream carbon is added as a precursor. Scrap (CN 7204) is outside CBAM scope and is not a precursor.

For iron & steel, only direct emissions are counted initially; electricity is excluded from the direct SEE. Confirm against Annex II / IR (EU) 2025/2547 before filing.
