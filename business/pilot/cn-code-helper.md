# CN Code Helper — Common Steel & Aluminium Codes

**Purpose:** quick reference for mapping products to **CN codes** in the pilot. Not exhaustive and
not legal advice — **always verify the exact code against Annex I of Reg. (EU) 2023/956** and the
customer's customs invoices.

## Rules

- A slightly different code can move a product **in or out of scope** — never guess.
- Confirm with **customs/export invoices** and the customer's product classification.
- Record ambiguity in `gap-register.md` (CN ambiguity).
- Exclusions (e.g. most ferro-alloys, ferrous scrap) matter — check them.
