# Source Stream Inventory — Phase 3

**Purpose:** a **structured checklist** of possible source streams, so the customer marks what
applies and supplies documents. This prevents the two failure modes: open-ended “send everything”
(noise) and self-selected lists (omissions).

> Customer marks **Used / Not used / Unknown**; we validate completeness against the route and the
> process flow. Customer confirms the final inventory.

## Instructions for the customer

For each row: mark whether the stream is used at the installation in the reporting period. If
used, provide the document(s) and the meter/source. If unsure, mark **Unknown** — we will help.
